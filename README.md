# History of Pakistan — On the Map

A 76-second vertical (9:16) map video telling 5,000 years of this land's history, from the Indus Valley Civilisation to Pakistan today.

- **Format:** vertical short video, voiceover only — no background music
- **Maps:** drawn from real country borders; historical shading uses today's borders as a guide and is approximate for old eras
- **Contents:** `SCRIPT.md` — the full voiceover script, scene by scene

## Scenes
1. Hook — "This map was not drawn in one day"
2. c. 3300–1300 BCE — Indus Valley: Mohenjo-daro & Harappa
3. 711 CE — Muhammad bin Qasim reaches Sindh
4. 1526–1857 — The Mughal era
5. 1857–1947 — British India
6. 14 August 1947 — Pakistan is born, in two wings
7. 1971 — East wing becomes Bangladesh
8. Today — four provinces, 240+ million people
