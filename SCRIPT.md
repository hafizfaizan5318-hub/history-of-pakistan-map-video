# Voiceover Script — History of Pakistan on the Map
Short vertical video (~75 seconds). Voiceover only, no music.

1. **Hook / title** — "This is Pakistan. But this map was not drawn in one day. It was shaped over five thousand years. Watch."
2. **c. 3300–1300 BCE — Indus Valley** — "Five thousand years ago, one of the world's first great civilisations grew here, along the Indus River. Mohenjo-daro. Harappa. Cities of brick, drains and trade."
3. **711 CE** — "In the year 711, Muhammad bin Qasim reached Sindh. Islam entered the subcontinent through this land, first."
4. **1526–1857 — Mughal era** — "For three hundred years, this land was the rich western heart of the Mughal Empire. Lahore. Multan. Thatta."
5. **1857–1947 — British rule** — "Then came the British. For ninety years the whole region was a single colony: British India."
6. **14 August 1947** — "Fourteenth August, 1947. The map is cut. Pakistan is born, in two wings: West and East, separated by sixteen hundred kilometres of India."
7. **1971** — "1971. After a war, the eastern wing becomes a new country: Bangladesh. And Pakistan takes the shape you see today."
8. **Today** — "Today: four provinces, and over two hundred and forty million people, between Iran, Afghanistan, China and India. Five thousand years. One map. This is Pakistan. Follow for more map stories."

## Production notes
- Each scene is a map image with a slow zoom; captions burned into each frame.
- Historical shading uses today's borders as a guide (approximate for old eras).
